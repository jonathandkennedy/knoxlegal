Attorney Rachel Knox

Florida Probate & Estate Administration

For nearly three decades, Rachel Knox has guided Florida families through probate with clarity, compassion, and determination. A 1997 graduate of the University of Miami School of Law, she is known for untangling the estates other lawyers turn away — tracing property interests, resolving title defects, locating hidden assets, and negotiating creditor claims that threaten a family's inheritance.

Her philosophy is simple: probate should bring closure, not conflict.
