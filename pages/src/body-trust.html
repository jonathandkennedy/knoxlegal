Trust questions

I'm the successor trustee. What do I do first?
Secure the assets, locate the trust document and any will, and get the required beneficiary notices out — Florida puts deadlines on trustee disclosures. Bring us in before the first notice and the entire administration starts clean.
The trustee won't show me the trust. Can they do that?
If you're a qualified beneficiary, no — Florida entitles you to a copy of the trust and to accountings. We enforce those rights quickly; see Beneficiary Representation.
Does a trust mean we skip probate entirely?
Only for assets actually titled in the trust. Anything left in the decedent's individual name — a car, an overlooked account, sometimes the house — still needs probate. We check, and handle both sides if needed.
